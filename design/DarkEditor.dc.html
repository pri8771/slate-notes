Trail mix recipe
Almonds, dried mango, dark chocolate chips, a pinch of sea salt. Toast the almonds first — ten minutes at 160°, shake the tray halfway. Ratio that worked: two parts nuts, one part fruit, half part chocolate. Store in the glass jar, not the tin — it goes stale in the tin. Try next time: smoked paprika on the almonds before toasting.
